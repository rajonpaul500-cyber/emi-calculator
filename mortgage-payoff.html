Mortgage Payoff Calculator FAQ

How much faster will extra payments pay off my mortgage?▶
Every extra rupee reduces your principal, which cuts future interest. Even a small monthly extra payment can shave years off a long mortgage. The comparison above shows the exact effect.
Should I make extra payments or invest the money?▶
If your mortgage rate is higher than the return you can earn safely on investments, paying down the mortgage is usually better. Otherwise, many people prefer to invest while keeping the lower mortgage payment.

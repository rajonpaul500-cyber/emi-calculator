Mortgage Calculator FAQ

What is included in my monthly mortgage payment?▶
Your total payment includes the EMI (principal and interest), property tax, home insurance, private mortgage insurance (PMI) when required, and any HOA fees — sometimes called PITI.
Should I choose a 15-year or 30-year mortgage?▶
A 30-year term has a lower monthly payment but much higher total interest. A 15-year term costs more per month but saves a large amount of interest and builds equity faster.
When does PMI go away?▶
PMI is generally removed automatically once you reach 22% equity and can be cancelled at 20% equity by requesting it from your lender.

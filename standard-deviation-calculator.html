Sample vs. Population Standard Deviation

Use Sample Standard Deviation (s) when your numbers represent a subset drawn from a larger population (standard in science labs and surveys; divides by N - 1). Use Population Standard Deviation (σ) when your dataset contains every single member of the group (divides by N).

Frequently asked questions

Why does Sample SD divide by N - 1 instead of N?▶
Dividing by N - 1 (Bessel's correction) removes downward bias when estimating true population spread from a smaller sample.

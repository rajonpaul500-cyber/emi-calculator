Why Inflation Must Factor Into Retirement & Savings

Even a modest 3.2% annual inflation rate doubles living expenses every ~22 years. Keeping long-term savings in a 0% checking account guarantees steady purchasing-power erosion. Pair this tool with the Compound Interest Calculator to compare nominal vs. inflation-adjusted returns.

Over the past century, US Consumer Price Index (CPI) inflation has averaged roughly 3.0% to 3.3% per year.

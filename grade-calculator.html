How Final Exam Targets Are Solved

Before finals week, multiply each completed category score by its syllabus weight, divide by completed weight to get your standing average, and solve directly for the minimum final exam score needed for an A, B, or passing threshold.

Frequently asked questions

What if the final exam needed score is over 100%?▶
A required score above 100% indicates the target grade is mathematically unreachable without extra credit—adjust your target slightly to see what grade is realistic.

FAQ

How do you simplify a ratio?▶
Divide both parts by their greatest common factor (GCF). For example 12:18 simplifies to 2:3 because the GCF is 6.
What is a ratio?▶
A ratio compares two quantities. 4:2 = 2:1 means both sides can be divided by the same number to create equal ratios.

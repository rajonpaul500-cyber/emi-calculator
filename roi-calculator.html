ROI vs annualized ROI

₹5,00,000 growing to ₹8,00,000 in 3 years is a 60% total ROI, but an annualized ROI of about 17%. Always compare annualized returns when investments have different holding periods.

ROI FAQ

Should ROI include taxes and fees?▶
For an honest number, yes. Subtract transaction costs, taxes and any additional expenses from the final value before calculating ROI.
Is higher ROI always better?▶
Not necessarily — higher returns usually mean higher risk. Consider both ROI and risk, and compare with the return of safer alternatives.

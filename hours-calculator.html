Whether you bill freelance hours or verify a paycheck, converting daily timecards from HH:MM into decimal payroll hours avoids rounding losses. Any shift where clock-out crosses midnight is handled cleanly too.

Frequently asked questions

How are unpaid meal breaks handled?▶
Each day's break minutes are subtracted directly from the elapsed duration between clock-in and clock-out.
How is overtime pay calculated?▶
Hours exceeding your weekly threshold (40 hours by default) are multiplied by 1.5x your base hourly rate.

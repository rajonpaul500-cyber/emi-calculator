How the U.S. Navy Body Fat Formula Works

Unlike standard scale weight or BMI, the U.S. Navy circumference formula isolates abdominal girth relative to neck circumference and height (plus hips for women). Because visceral and subcutaneous trunk fat expand the waist much faster than neck tissue, a single tape measure estimates body fat within roughly 3%–4% of DEXA scans.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I measure my waist for the Navy formula?▶
Men should measure horizontally right across the belly button (navel). Women should measure at the narrowest natural waist point, plus the widest point around the hips and glutes.
What is lean body mass?▶
Lean body mass equals total body weight minus fat weight—including skeletal muscle, bones, organs, and water.

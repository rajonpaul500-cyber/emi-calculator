FAQ

What is the Greatest Common Factor?▶
The GCF is the largest positive integer that divides all given numbers. For example the GCF of 36 and 48 is 12.
What are factors?▶
Factors are numbers that divide into a number exactly. The GCF gives the biggest common factor across numbers.

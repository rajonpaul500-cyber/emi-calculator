FAQ

What is the quadratic formula?▶
x = (-b +/- sqrt(b^2 - 4ac)) / (2a). The discriminant (b^2 - 4ac) determines the nature of the roots.
What does the discriminant tell us?▶
If the discriminant is positive, there are two real roots. If zero, one real root. If negative, two complex roots.

Stacked Discounts: 25% off + extra 10% off = 32.5% total off (not 35%)
Formula: Sale = Original x (1 - d1) x (1 - d2)

Why Stacked Discounts Don't Simply Add Together

When a retailer advertises "25% off plus an extra 10% off," the second discount applies to the already-reduced price—not the original tag. On a $100 item, 25% off drops the price to $75, and 10% off $75 saves $7.50, making the final pre-tax price $67.50 (a true 32.5% discount).

Frequently asked questions

Does a 20% + 20% discount equal 40% off?▶
No. Applying 20% leaves 80%, and taking another 20% off 80% leaves 64%, which is a 36% total discount.

Escaping the High-APR Minimum Payment Trap

Credit cards carry some of the highest interest rates in consumer finance (19%–29% APR). Whenever your monthly payment barely clears monthly accrued interest, principal reduction stalls. Read our Debt Snowball vs. Debt Avalanche Guide to eliminate balances systematically.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if my monthly payment is lower than the first month's interest?▶
Your balance grows instead of shrinking—negative amortization. Increasing your payment above the monthly interest charge is essential.

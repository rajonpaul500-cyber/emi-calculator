Avoiding the Common Reverse Sales Tax Mistake

If an item costs $108 including 8% tax, multiplying $108 by 8% incorrectly yields $8.64 instead of the true $8.00 tax. Always divide by (1 + tax rate) to isolate the genuine pre-tax base price.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reverse-calculate VAT or GST from a total?▶
Divide the gross receipt amount by (1 + VAT rate). For 15% VAT on a $115 receipt, the pre-tax price is $115 / 1.15 = $100.

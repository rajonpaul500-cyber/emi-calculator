How to Calculate Loan & Mortgage EMI

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed payment made to a lender each month until a loan is repaid in full. It combines principal repayment and monthly interest using the standard amortization formula:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n / ((1 + r)n − 1)

Where P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the total number of monthly payments. Open the EMI Calculator or Mortgage Calculator to inspect your complete amortization schedule and extra-payment savings.

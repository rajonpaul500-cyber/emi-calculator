FAQ

What is an exponent?▶
An exponent tells you how many times to multiply the base by itself. For example 2^3 = 2 * 2 * 2 = 8.
What about fractional or negative exponents?▶
A fractional exponent (like 1/2) is a root. A negative exponent means 1 divided by the positive power.

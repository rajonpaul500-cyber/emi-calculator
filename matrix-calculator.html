FAQ

How do you add matrices?▶
Add matching elements: A[i][j] + B[i][j]. Both matrices must have the same dimensions.
How is matrix multiplication done?▶
Row of A multiplied with column of B. The result cell at (i,j) is the sum of products of corresponding elements.

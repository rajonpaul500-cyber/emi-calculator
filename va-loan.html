VA loan advantages

  • No down payment — most VA loans require 0% down.
  • No PMI — VA loans never carry private mortgage insurance.
  • Funding fee — a one-time fee (1.4%–3.3%) that can be financed into the loan; it is waived for disabled veterans.

VA Mortgage FAQ

Who can get a VA loan?▶
Active duty service members, veterans, National Guard and Reserve members, and some surviving spouses who meet service requirements are eligible for VA-backed home loans.
Can the VA funding fee be waived?▶
Yes — the funding fee is waived for veterans receiving VA disability compensation and for surviving spouses of veterans who died in service or from a service-connected disability.

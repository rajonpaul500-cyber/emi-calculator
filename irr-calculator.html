How IRR is computed

IRR finds the rate r where NPV = −Investment + Σ Cash Flow ÷ (1+r)^year + Residual ÷ (1+r)^years = 0. The calculator solves this equation numerically. If no positive rate makes the equation balance, the result shows N/A — the cash flows are not enough to recover the investment.

IRR FAQ

What is a good IRR?▶
Compare IRR to your required rate of return or the cost of capital. In India, projects with an IRR above 12-15% are generally considered attractive, but it depends on risk.
Can IRR be negative?▶
Yes, if the cash flows fail to return the initial investment over time. A negative IRR means the project loses money even before considering inflation.

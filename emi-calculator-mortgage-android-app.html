02. The Exact Banking Formula Used by the App

Commercial banks, housing finance institutions, and credit unions worldwide compute fixed monthly installments using the standard reducing-balance amortization equation:

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)n] ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1]

Where P = Principal loan amount, r = Monthly interest rate (Annual APR ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n = Total number of monthly installments (Years × 12).

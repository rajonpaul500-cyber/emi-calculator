Comparing Clinical Ideal Weight Formulas

The Devine formula remains the standard baseline used in hospitals for medical dosing, while Robinson and Miller adjusted coefficients to reflect modern healthy body frames. Rather than aiming for one exact pound or kilogram, most nutritionists recommend targeting a comfortable weight inside the WHO healthy BMI range.

Frequently asked questions

Why do Devine, Robinson, Miller, and Hamwi give slightly different weights?▶
Each formula was calibrated on different clinical datasets between 1964 and 1983. Viewing all four together gives a realistic target band rather than a rigid single figure.
Does ideal weight account for muscle frame?▶
Standard IBW formulas only take height and sex as inputs. Athletes with higher lean mass naturally sit near or above the upper end of the range.

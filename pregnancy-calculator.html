How Estimated Due Dates Are Calculated

Obstetricians calculate standard due dates by adding 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of your last menstrual period (adjusted if your cycle differs from 28 days). Only about 4% of babies arrive on the exact predicted day—most healthy full-term births occur between weeks 37 and 42.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is an LMP-based due date?▶
It gives a reliable baseline within about 1–2 weeks if menstrual cycles are regular. A first-trimester dating ultrasound between weeks 8 and 13 provides the highest clinical precision.
How are IVF transfer dates calculated?▶
For a 3-day transfer, gestational age begins 17 days prior to transfer (add 263 days for due date). For a 5-day blastocyst, add 261 days from transfer.

FAQ

What is half-life?▶
Half-life is the time taken for half of a radioactive substance (or any decaying quantity) to decay or disintegrate.
How is half-life calculated from remaining amount?▶
Use the exponential decay formula: t(1/2) = (time * ln(2)) / ln(initial / remaining).

FAQ

What is the Least Common Multiple?▶
The LCM of two or more numbers is the smallest number that is a common multiple of all of them. It is used to find common denominators.
How do I find LCM?▶
Find the GCF first. LCM = |a * b| / GCF(a,b). For more than two numbers, repeat the process.

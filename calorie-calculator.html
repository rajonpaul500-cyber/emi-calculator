Understanding BMR vs. TDEE

Your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the energy your body burns purely to stay alive at complete rest. Your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) multiplies BMR by your daily movement and exercise factor. Eating at TDEE maintains weight; eating 500 kcal below TDEE yields roughly 1 lb (0.45 kg) of fat loss per week.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the Mifflin-St Jeor calorie formula?▶
Clinical nutrition studies consistently rank Mifflin-St Jeor as the most accurate standard equation for predicting resting calorie burn within +/- 10% for most non-athlete adults.
What is the minimum safe daily calorie intake?▶
Most medical guidelines advise not dropping below 1,200 kcal/day for women or 1,500 kcal/day for men without direct physician supervision, as steeper deficits risk muscle loss and nutrient deficiency.
How many calories equal one pound of body weight?▶
Roughly 3,500 calories equals one pound (0.454 kg) of fat tissue. A 500-calorie daily deficit creates a 3,500-calorie weekly deficit, targeting about 1 pound per week.

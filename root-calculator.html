What is a root?

The nth root of a number x is the value that, multiplied by itself n times, gives x. For example the 4th root of 16 is 2 because 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 16. The square root (n = 2) and cube root (n = 3) are the most common.

Roots and exponents are inverse operations: the nth root of x equals x^(1/n). A fractional exponent like 1/2 is another way of writing a square root.

Simplified radical form

Many roots are not whole numbers, but they can still be written exactly. For example √72 = 6√2 because 72 = 36 × 2 and √36 = 6. This 'simplified radical form' keeps answers exact instead of rounding.

The calculator extracts the largest perfect power from under the radical. It also handles odd roots of negative numbers, which are defined for real numbers (for example ∛(-8) = -2).

Frequently asked questions

What is the nth root?▶
The nth root of x is the number y such that yⁿ = x. For example the 4th root of 16 is 2 because 2⁴ = 16.
How do I find the square root?▶
Enter the number and set the root to 2. For example √144 = 12.
What is √72 simplified?▶
Since 72 = 36 × 2 and √36 = 6, the simplified form is 6√2 ≈ 8.485.
Can I take the cube root of a negative number?▶
Yes. Odd roots of negative numbers are real: ∛(-8) = -2 because (-2)³ = -8. Even roots of negatives are not real.
What is the difference between a root and an exponent?▶
They are inverses. The nth root of x equals x^(1/n). Raising to the power n undoes taking the nth root.

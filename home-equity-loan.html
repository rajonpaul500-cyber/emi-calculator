Home Equity Loan Calculator FAQ

How much home equity can I borrow?▶
Lenders typically allow you to borrow up to 80% of your home's value, minus your existing mortgage balance. Your remaining equity backs the loan, which is why rates are usually lower than personal loans.
What happens if I can't repay my home equity loan?▶
Your home is the collateral, so failure to repay can lead to foreclosure. Only borrow what you can comfortably afford, and use the extra payment feature here to plan a faster payoff.

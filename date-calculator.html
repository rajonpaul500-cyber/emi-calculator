Counting Business Days vs. Calendar Days

Project deadlines, shipping estimates, and legal notice periods frequently specify either calendar days or business days (Monday through Friday). Switching between both modes lets you calculate contract deadlines or travel itineraries in seconds.

Frequently asked questions

Does the business day count exclude Saturdays and Sundays?▶
Yes. Weekdays Monday through Friday are counted as business days, while Saturdays and Sundays are tallied separately.
How does adding months handle month ends like January 31 + 1 month?▶
JavaScript date rolling adjusts overflow automatically; for fixed-day deadlines, adding exact days or weeks avoids calendar ambiguity.

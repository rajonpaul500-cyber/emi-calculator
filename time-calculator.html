Adding Hours, Minutes & Seconds Without Base-10 Errors

Because time uses base-60 arithmetic for seconds and minutes and base-24 for days, standard calculators produce misleading decimals (for example 1.45 + 0.35 = 1.80 instead of 2:20). Converting every unit into total seconds first guarantees error-free rollover.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert hours and minutes to decimal hours?▶
Divide minutes by 60 and add to hours. For example, 7 hours 45 minutes = 7 + 45/60 = 7.75 decimal hours.

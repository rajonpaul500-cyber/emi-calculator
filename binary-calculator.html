FAQ

How do I add binary numbers?▶
Line them up and add digit by digit right to left. If the sum is 2, write 0 and carry 1 to the left. Example: 101 + 11 = 1000.
What can I do with a binary calculator?▶
Add, subtract, multiply, divide binary numbers, or convert between binary and decimal.

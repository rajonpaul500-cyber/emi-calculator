How the payback period works

Divide the investment by the annual cash flow to find full years, then the leftover is expressed in months. This simple rule of thumb is great for quickly ranking projects — the shorter the payback, the lower the risk of the money being tied up.

Payback Period FAQ

Should I prefer a shorter payback period?▶
Usually yes, because recovering money faster reduces risk. But a longer-payback project with high returns after recovery can still be the better overall investment.
Does the calculator consider the time value of money?▶
No. This is a simple payback calculation. For a discounted payback or return analysis, use the IRR or Finance calculators.

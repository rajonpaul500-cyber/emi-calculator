What Is Basal Metabolic Rate?

Your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) represents the baseline calories your organs, brain, heart, and muscles require over 24 hours purely to sustain breathing, circulation, and cellular repair. Even if you stayed in bed all day, your body would burn roughly your BMR.

Frequently asked questions

Which BMR equation should I use?▶
Use Mifflin-St Jeor for general adults, and use Katch-McArdle if you already know your body fat percentage accurately and carry above-average muscle mass.
Should I eat below my BMR to lose weight?▶
Most coaches recommend eating below TDEE rather than drastically below BMR. Going far beneath BMR for extended periods increases fatigue and lean muscle loss.

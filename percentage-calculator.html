Understanding percentages

A percentage is a fraction with a denominator of 100. Saying 20% of 250 means 20/100 of 250, which equals 50. Percentages make comparisons easy because they put every quantity on the same 100-point scale.

There are three classic percentage questions: finding a part of a whole (what is 15% of 80), finding a share (what percent of 80 is 12), and measuring change (from 50 to 60 is a 20% increase). This calculator answers all three, plus increase and decrease.

Percentage change explained

Percentage change measures how much a value moved relative to its starting point: (new - old) / old × 100. A positive result is an increase, a negative result is a decrease.

For example, if a price rises from $50 to $65, the change is (65 - 50)/50 × 100 = 30%. If it falls from $65 back to $50, the change is (50 - 65)/65 × 100 = -23.1%. Notice the percentages differ because the starting points differ.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?▶
Multiply the number by the percentage divided by 100. For example 20% of 250 = 250 × 20/100 = 50.
How do I calculate percentage change?▶
Use the formula (new - old) / old × 100. From 50 to 60 that is (60-50)/50 × 100 = 20%, an increase.
What percent is 25 of 200?▶
Divide 25 by 200 and multiply by 100: 25/200 × 100 = 12.5%.
How do I add a percentage to a number?▶
Multiply the number by (1 + percentage/100). Adding 15% to 80 gives 80 × 1.15 = 92.

How amortization works

Each payment first covers the interest that accrued on the remaining balance, and the rest reduces the principal. Early in the schedule most of your payment is interest; near the end most of it is principal. Extra payments accelerate this by reducing the balance faster, which is why the schedule above ends early when extras are added.

Amortization Calculator FAQ

What is the difference between amortization and interest rate?▶
The interest rate determines how much interest accrues. Amortization describes the payment schedule that gradually pays down the principal while covering that interest.
Can I download the amortization schedule?▶
Yes, click the "Download CSV" button in the result panel to export the full schedule to Excel or Google Sheets.

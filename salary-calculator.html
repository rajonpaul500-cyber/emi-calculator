Converting Between Hourly Wages and Annual Salaries

Assuming a standard 40-hour workweek across 52 weeks (2,080 work hours per year), a $35/hour wage translates to $72,800 per year before taxes. If you are a contractor without paid holidays, subtracting 15 unpaid days shows your true holiday-adjusted annual take-home run rate.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours are in a full-time work year?▶
At 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, a standard work year has 2,080 hours.
What is the difference between biweekly and semi-monthly pay?▶
Biweekly pays every 2 weeks (26 paychecks per year), while semi-monthly pays twice a month on fixed dates (24 paychecks per year).

Personalized Hydration vs. the 8-Glass Myth

The popular "8 glasses a day" guideline ignores body size and sweat loss. A 55 kg office worker and a 95 kg athlete training outdoors require noticeably different fluid volumes. Scaling intake by roughly 35 ml per kilogram plus workout sweat replenishment keeps electrolytes and energy steady.

Frequently asked questions

Does coffee or tea count toward daily water intake?▶
Yes. Plain coffee, herbal tea, milk, and high-water fruits and vegetables all contribute toward total daily fluid balance.
How do I know if I am properly hydrated?▶
Pale straw-colored urine and steady daytime energy are reliable practical indicators of adequate hydration.

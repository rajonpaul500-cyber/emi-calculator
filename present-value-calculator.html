Why discounting matters

$10,00,000 received 10 years from now is worth only about $5,50,000 today at a 6% discount rate. The higher the rate or the longer the wait, the less a future amount is worth now.

Present Value FAQ

What discount rate should I use?▶
Use the return you could earn elsewhere (opportunity cost) or your cost of borrowing. For safe cash flows, a 5-8% rate is common in India.
What is the difference between present value and future value?▶
Future value grows a current amount forward with compounding. Present value discounts a future amount backward. They are two sides of the same coin.

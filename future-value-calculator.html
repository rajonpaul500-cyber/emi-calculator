The power of compounding

Future Value FAQ

What is the rule of 72?▶
Divide 72 by your annual rate to estimate how long money takes to double. At 7%, it doubles roughly every 10.3 years — matching this example.
Does inflation reduce future value?▶
Yes — future value is nominal. To see real purchasing power, subtract inflation from your rate (e.g., 7% return minus 6% inflation is about 1% real growth).

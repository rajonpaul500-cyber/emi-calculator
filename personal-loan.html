Personal Loan Calculator FAQ

What is an origination fee?
An origination fee is a one-time charge lenders deduct upfront. If you borrow ₹1,00,000 with a 2% fee, you receive ₹98,000 but repay the loan on the full ₹1,00,000, so the fee adds to your total cost.
Why is the amount I receive less than the loan amount?
Because the origination fee is deducted before disbursement. Always check both the loan amount and the amount you actually receive when comparing lenders.
Should I use extra payments for my personal loan?
If you can afford it, extra payments reduce your balance faster, shorten the loan term, and save a significant amount of interest — especially on higher-rate personal loans.

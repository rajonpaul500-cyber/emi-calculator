How FHA insurance works

FHA loans charge two insurance premiums:

  • Upfront MIP (1.75%) — paid once, usually added to your loan balance, so the total loan is slightly larger than the home price minus down payment.
  • Annual MIP (about 0.55%) — paid monthly as part of your payment, and unlike private PMI, it typically lasts for the life of the loan.

FHA Loan FAQ

Who is an FHA loan good for?▶
FHA loans are popular with first-time buyers because of the low 3.5% down payment and more flexible credit requirements. The trade-off is lifetime mortgage insurance.
Can FHA MIP be removed?▶
For most FHA loans made after June 2013 with less than 10% down, MIP stays for the life of the loan. The only way to remove it is to refinance into a conventional loan.

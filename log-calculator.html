FAQ

What is a logarithm?▶
A logarithm answers "to what exponent must the base be raised to produce this number?" For example log(100) base 10 = 2 because 10^2 = 100.
What is the natural log?▶
The natural logarithm (ln) uses base e (about 2.71828). It is widely used in science and finance.

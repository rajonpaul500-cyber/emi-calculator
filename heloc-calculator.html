How HELOC payments work

During the draw period (typically 10 years) you may borrow repeatedly up to your limit. With interest-only payments, you pay just the monthly interest — small payments, but the balance stays full. In the repayment period, you can no longer borrow and payments jump because you repay principal plus interest over the remaining term.

HELOC Calculator FAQ

Are HELOC rates fixed or variable?▶
Most HELOCs use a variable rate tied to a benchmark index, so your payment can change. Some lenders offer fixed-rate options for a portion of the balance.
Why is my HELOC payment so much higher after the draw period?▶
Because you stop paying interest-only and start repaying the principal too, over a shorter time. This calculator shows both payments so you can plan for the jump.

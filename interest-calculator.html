Simple vs compound interest

Simple interest: only the original principal earns interest. Great for short periods and simple loans.

Compound interest: interest earns interest. Choose a compounding frequency to see the effective annual rate — continuous compounding gives the highest return, followed by daily, monthly and quarterly.

Interest Calculator FAQ

What is the effective annual rate?▶
It is the real yearly rate after compounding. With 6% nominal interest compounded monthly, the effective annual rate is about 6.17% because interest earns interest every month.
Which compounding frequency gives the highest return?▶
Continuous compounding gives the highest return, then daily, monthly, quarterly, semi-annually and annually. The differences shrink as rates get lower.

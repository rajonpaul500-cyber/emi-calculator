How Exact Age Is Computed Across Leap Years

Calendar months vary between 28, 29, 30, and 31 days. Rather than approximating a year as 365.25 days, this calculator steps month-by-month across actual Gregorian calendar boundaries while counting exact elapsed days (including every February 29 leap day) for total weeks, days, and hours.

Frequently asked questions

Does the age calculator include leap years?▶
Yes. Every February 29 between your birth date and target date is counted accurately in total days, weeks, and hours lived.
Can I calculate someone's age on a past or future date?▶
Yes. Change the 'Age at the Date of' input to any historical or future calendar date.

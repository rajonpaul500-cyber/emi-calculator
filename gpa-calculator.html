How College GPA Is Weighted by Credit Hours

Every course contributes grade points equal to its numeric grade value (A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0) multiplied by its credit weight. A 4-credit science lab shifts your semester average twice as much as a 2-credit seminar.

Frequently asked questions

What grade values are used on the 4.0 scale?▶
A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B- = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, C- = 1.7, D+ = 1.3, D = 1.0, F = 0.0.
How do I raise my cumulative GPA fastest?▶
Earning A grades in higher-credit core courses raises cumulative quality points faster when total prior credits are still moderate.

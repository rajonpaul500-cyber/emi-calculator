03. Smart Study Strategy to Score GPA 5.00 (Golden A+) in HSC

  1. Lock In Compulsory ICT & English Early: ICT covers Number Systems (Binary & Hexadecimal), Digital Logic Gates, HTML, and C Programming. Mastering ICT and English grammar guarantees two effortless A+ grades across every group.
  2. Solve 5 Years of Board Questions Per Chapter: After reading a chapter in the HSC Guide All app, immediately practice the past 5 years of board MCQs and CQs to recognize recurring stem patterns.
  3. Use Your 4th Subject as an A+ Insurance Policy: Scoring an A+ (5.00) in your optional 4th subject adds +3.00 bonus grade points, which can lift two A grades (4.00) in main subjects all the way up to a full GPA 5.00.

How is HSC GPA 5.00 calculated with the 4th (Optional) subject in Bangladesh?▶
In the HSC 5.00 grading scale, your 6 main subjects are averaged after adding bonus points from your 4th (optional) subject. Any grade points above 2.00 in your 4th subject (up to +3.00 bonus points for an A+) are added to your total grade points before dividing by 6, capped at GPA 5.00.

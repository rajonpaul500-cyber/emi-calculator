Why Cryptographic Entropy Beats Predictable Patterns

Modern GPU cracking rigs test tens of billions of dictionary combinations per second. A 16-character password drawn uniformly from 90+ characters provides over 100 bits of entropy, making brute-force guessing computationally infeasible.

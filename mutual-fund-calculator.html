SIP vs lump sum

A SIP spreads your purchases across market ups and downs, reducing timing risk. A lump sum invests everything at once, which can do better in rising markets but carries more timing risk. Many investors combine both — exactly what this calculator models.

Mutual Fund FAQ

Are mutual fund returns guaranteed?▶
No. Equity fund returns depend on the market. The calculator gives an estimate based on the return you enter, not a promise.
How long should I stay invested?▶
Longer horizons (7+ years) historically smooth out market volatility and improve the chance of positive returns. Staying invested through dips usually beats trying to time the market.

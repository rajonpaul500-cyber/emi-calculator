How to Use Pace Splits for Race Day

Consistent pacing prevents burning out in the first half of a race. Entering your target finish time for a 5K, 10K, or Marathon instantly gives you exact per-kilometer and per-mile split checkpoints so you can keep steady cadence on your GPS watch.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between pace and speed?▶
Pace measures time per unit distance (e.g. 5:00 min/km), while speed measures distance per unit time (e.g. 12.0 km/h).
What is a good beginner 5K pace?▶
Most first-time 5K runners finish between 30 and 38 minutes, corresponding to a comfortable pace of 6:00 to 7:35 minutes per kilometer.
